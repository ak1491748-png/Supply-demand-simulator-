Supply & Demand Characteristics

Demand: The demand curve slopes downward. As price increases, quantity demanded generally decreases.

Supply: The supply curve slopes upward. As price increases, quantity supplied generally increases.

Equilibrium: The market is in equilibrium where quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

Curve shifts: Non-price factors shift the entire curve, while a change in the good's own price creates movement along a curve.
